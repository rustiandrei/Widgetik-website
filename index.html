Questions, answered

Is Widgetik free?

Yes. Widgetik is free to download and includes ads. Premium is an optional one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks Agenda, Upcoming Birthdays, Habit Counter, Focus Timer, Zen Garden and extra fonts.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 9 or later.

How do I add a widget?

On iPhone and iPad, touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Widgetik. On Android, touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Widgets, find Widgetik and drag a widget into place. More help on the Support page.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Your widgets and their data are stored on your device.

If I buy Premium on iPhone, do I get it on Android too?

No. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and stay on that platform. Within a platform, Premium follows your Apple Account or Google account to your other devices.

Which languages does Widgetik speak?

English, Chinese, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Ukrainian, and on iPhone and iPad also Arabic, Hindi and Vietnamese.
