Widgets

How do I add a widget on iPhone or iPad?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, then tap Edit and Add Widget. Search for Widgetik, swipe to the widget and size you want, and tap Add Widget.

How do I add a widget on Android?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen and tap Widgets. Find Widgetik, then drag a widget onto your Home Screen. Tap the widget to customize it. To change its size, touch and hold it and drag its edges.

How do I change how a widget looks?

Open Widgetik and choose the widget. Pick a theme, colors, a background and fonts; the live preview shows each change, and your Home Screen widget updates to match.

On iPhone and iPad, if you have more than one of the same widget, such as two habit widgets, touch and hold a widget and tap Edit Widget to choose which one it shows.

How do I add Lock Screen widgets or Control Center controls on iPhone?

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen. Tap the widget area and choose Widgetik.

Control Center: open Control Center, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Add a Control and search for Widgetik.

How do I add the Quick Settings tiles on Android?

Swipe down twice from the top of the screen to open Quick Settings, then tap Edit (the pencil). Drag Widgetik's Log Water or Focus Timer tile into your tiles.

My steps don't show up.

iPhone and iPad: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps under Privacy. Choose Widgetik and turn on Steps.

Android: open Health Connect from your device's settings, choose Widgetik under App permissions and allow Steps. Widgetik shows the steps that your phone or your fitness apps save to Health Connect.

Agenda doesn't show my events.

Widgetik needs permission to read your calendar. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars > Widgetik and choose Full Access. On Android, go to Settings > Apps > Widgetik > Permissions and allow Calendar. Then open Widgetik and check which calendars Agenda shows.

Premium

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes ads and unlocks Agenda, Upcoming Birthdays, Habit Counter, Focus Timer, Zen Garden and extra fonts. On iPhone and iPad it also unlocks custom text colors and Transparent Backgrounds. On Android, Premium is called Widgetik PRO.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

iPhone and iPad: sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with, open the Premium screen in Widgetik and tap Restore Purchase.

Android: sign in to Google Play with the account you bought it with, then open Widgetik while you're online. Widgetik checks with Google Play and unlocks PRO on its own.

I bought Premium on iPhone. Do I get it on Android?

No. The App Store and Google Play are separate, so a purchase on one doesn't unlock the other.

Privacy

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There's no account and no Widgetik server. Deleting the app deletes its data, except water you saved to Apple Health or Health Connect, which stays there until you delete it. Read the privacy policy.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium removes them. Where ad consent applies, you can change your choice from Privacy options in the app.
